What is SITAM?

It started in Padova in 1946. The Padovani family, tailors for generations, invented a curved rule that draws a pattern straight from your own measurements. Simple, fast, and it fits every body, because it was never made for a standard one. Anna learned it at sixteen and has taught it for forty years.
