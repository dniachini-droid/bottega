# The prompts nobody measures

## What it does

Finds out how other people write and maintain the prompt that starts a working
session — what goes in, what stays out, how it is kept short, and how it is
generated rather than typed. Produces one research page and a recommendation.
It builds nothing.

## Why now

This workshop polices its own instruction files to the character. A building
session is held to 10,000 tokens and spent today arguing over 515 of them.

**Then the guide window hands that session a prompt of 27,400 tokens** — three
times the entire budget, written by hand, every time, and counted by nothing.
Six limits, and not one of them looks at the largest single thing a session
receives.

## What done looks like

- **`docs/research/PROMPTS.md`** exists, following `docs/research/README.md`:
  research is reference not authority, and every claim marked as read in full
  or taken only from a search summary.
- **A survey of several real approaches**, named, with where each comes from —
  what people actually run, not only what is written up.
- **Five questions answered, with evidence:**
  1. **What belongs in a dispatch prompt, and what does not?** This workshop's
     rule is "put the facts in, not directions to the facts" — because a fact
     a session must go and find costs accuracy. Is that right, and where does
     it stop being right?
  2. **Is there a length at which a prompt starts costing more than it buys?**
     Any measured relationship between prompt size and how well the work goes.
  3. **How is the prompt produced?** Typed by hand, filled from a template,
     generated from the state of the work, or assembled from pieces a session
     pulls only when it needs them.
  4. **What is kept out on purpose, and how?** Not by asking politely — by
     something that refuses.
  5. **What has been tried and abandoned?** The most useful answer and the
     hardest to find.
- **A recommendation for this workshop**, in plain words, that would actually
  have prevented a 27,400-token prompt.
- **It says whether the prompt should be measured**, and if so against what —
  a seventh limit is a real proposal and must be argued for, not assumed.

## What is out

- Building any of it. No rule, no skill, no template, no change to the check.
- Anything about memory. That is `docs/research/MEMORY.md` and pull request 8.
- Duplicating `docs/research/HANDOFF.md`, which covers **what may travel
  between sessions and what must not** — isolation, staleness, refusability.
  It says nothing about size or composition. Read it once, then stay off it.
- Zibaldone, the rename, and either 10,000 limit.
