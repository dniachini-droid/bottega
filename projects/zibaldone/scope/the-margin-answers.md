# The margin answers

## What it does

When he keeps a thought, the margin beside it fills in with the subject pages
that thought probably belongs to, before he puts the phone down. Each one is a
door into Codex, exactly as the settled entries already have.

Today the margin beside his newest line is the only empty one on the page.
Everything older shows where it went; the thought he wrote four seconds ago
shows nothing until the night. *Why that matters enough to build: the promise of
the whole product is switched on for the past and off for the present, and the
present is the only moment he is actually looking at the screen. A design
critique on 16 September 2026 named this the one thing worth more than every
other idea in four rounds.*

## The owner's decision, 16 September 2026, which does not move

**The app guesses from what it already holds. It never asks a model, and it
never calls out.** He was given both versions and chose this one in his own
words: *"cheap version only. Never expensive version."*

The app already keeps a copy of every subject page — title and summary — on its
own disk. Matching his new words against that copy is local, costs nothing, and
is too fast to perceive. The night pass then replaces the guess with the real
filing, which is a judgement made properly.

**What is refused, permanently:** a model call on the capture path; any outbound
request from the app; anything that makes him wait, costs money per capture, or
fails when he has no signal. *Why written as a refusal rather than a preference:
the expensive version is the one that will look tempting later, when the guess
is wrong and somebody wants it to be cleverer. The answer to a wrong guess is a
better local match or an honest mark, never a model.*

**The app has never once talked to the outside world.** That is checked, not
assumed — no address appears anywhere in `server/`. This piece must not be the
first thing to break it.

## What done looks like

- **The guess is visibly a guess.** The design already has a language for ink
  that has not settled, built as the `ink` idea. A margin that fills in at once
  and firms up overnight is honest about what it knows. *Why this is a
  requirement and not a nicety: a margin that confidently says a thought went to
  `Mother` when it did not is worse than an empty one — it is the product
  lying to him about his own mind.*
- **Where it has nothing to say, it says nothing** and waits for the night. A
  guess is offered only when the match is good enough to stand behind.
- **Capture is not slower.** The existing measurement holds: the write path is
  timed, and this piece does not move it.
- **The night pass overrules the guess** without ceremony. What the filing
  decides is what the margin ends up holding.
- **The doors it draws are reachable**, to the same standard the round-four
  fixes met: every one answers across a 24 by 24 square centred on it, with real
  separation between stacked doors.

## What is out

- **Any model, anywhere on this path.** Settled above.
- **Rewriting the filing.** The night pass is unchanged; this piece only fills
  the gap before it runs.
- **Guessing at anything but subjects.** No summary, no connection, no article.
