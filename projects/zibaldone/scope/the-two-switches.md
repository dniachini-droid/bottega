# The two switches

## What it does

Lets him turn the nights off, in two halves, from the notebook itself.

His words, 18 September 2026: *"Can we have a button in Zibaldone that says to
run or not to run the filing, codex and desengo? Like on and off switch? That
way when I haven't been writing in it, it doesn't do it."* And when asked
whether off meant the whole night or only the articles: *"2 switches. Filing
And codex articles / Desegno articles. I want both to turn on or off."*

- **The filing.** On, a night brings in what he has written and rewrites the
  Codex pages it touches. Off, it does not.
- **Disegno.** On, a night writes him an article. Off, it does not.

Both on by default, and they stay as he last left them.

*Why on the notebook rather than in the repository's settings: it is the thing
he has on his phone, and the reason he wants this is a week away from writing,
which is exactly when he is not at a computer.*

## Why he asked, which is not quite what the switches do

He asked because a night with nothing new in it still wrote him an article. He
is right that it did. On 17 September he had ruled the other way — *"NO, it
should NOT be declined"* — and this is him taking part of that back, which is
his to take back.

**But a switch he has to remember to flick is a worse answer than a rule that
knows, for that particular case**, and this page says so rather than pretending
otherwise. The switches are still worth building: they are for a fortnight
away, for a week he does not want looked at, for turning the whole thing off
without touching anything technical. Whether a night with nothing new should
also decline on its own is a separate question and is **not** settled here.

## What it must never do

- **Never lose anything while it is off.** What he writes waits, and the first
  night after he turns the filing back on brings in all of it. The filing
  already works this way; nothing here may change that.
- **Never make a deliberate pause look like a fault.** A night that stops
  because a switch is off finishes green and says which switch.
- **Never print anything of his** in a log: not a capture, an id, a page, a
  subject, or a word he wrote. The rule that already binds the clock.
- **Never write an article from a mind the filing has not caught up.** The
  guard built on 17 September stands, and these switches may not open a way
  round it.
- **Never let a switch be readable by anyone who cannot already open the
  notebook.**

## The case where the two switches disagree

**The filing off and Disegno on.** He has written things; the filing is not
allowed to bring them in; the noticing is asked to write.

The guard from 17 September already refuses this, because the notebook holds
captures the mind does not have — and it is right to. An article written from
a mind deliberately held behind is the same bad article as one written from a
mind accidentally held behind.

**So the refusal stands. What must change is what it says.** "File what's new
first, then notice" is useless advice to a man who has switched the filing off
on purpose. It has to say that: the filing is switched off, so there is nothing
new to write from, and here is which switch to change.

## What done looks like

- Two switches on the notebook, reachable from his phone, each on or off, both
  on until he changes them, and staying as he left them.
- A night reads both before it does anything, and the reading happens the way
  the filing already asks the notebook anything — no second way in, and nothing
  a stranger could read.
- The filing switched off: the night does not file, says so, and is green.
- Disegno switched off: the night does not write an article, says so, and is
  green.
- Both off: the night does nothing at all, says so, and is green.
- Nothing he wrote is lost while either is off, and the first night after the
  filing is switched back on brings in everything that waited. Watched, with a
  capture made while off and filed after.
- The filing off and Disegno on: no article is written, and what the run says
  names the switch rather than telling him to file what's new.
- The notebook unreachable or locked: a night does not stop on a question it
  could not ask, and says that it could not ask. The same answer the noticing
  already gives, for the same reason.
- Nothing in any log names a capture, an id, a page, a subject or a word of
  his.
- Every refusal above watched failing and then passing, written in
  `docs/REFUSALS.md`.
- The whole suite is green.
